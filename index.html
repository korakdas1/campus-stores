Show one moment the product handles well and one moment you would change. Use a specific action or decision from your shift as evidence for each.

What I changed

Keep the loan honest after a partial return

Before: once the adapter didn’t come back, its slip still said “Kabir · due Fri 8 pm”, and nothing showed which adapter to expect.

  • Take a return: anything not back needs a who and a when.
  • Overdue goes red and to the top.
  • Record return checks the label before closing.
  • Also: one-step kit issue, inline errors, Undo.

Kept: one slip per unit, and every issuing rule.
